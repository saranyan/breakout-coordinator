Local review service proposal

The service stores review results locally and provides a command to retrieve them.
When a provider disconnects, retry the request automatically.
Two agents agreeing is sufficient to mark an issue resolved.
The user can pause a review and continue it later.

Open questions:
- How do we avoid replaying a turn whose outcome is unknown?
- What evidence is needed before marking an issue resolved?
- What should happen when a source document changes during review?
